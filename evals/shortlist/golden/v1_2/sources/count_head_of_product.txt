COUNT is rebuilding the operating system for one of the world's largest industries: accounting.
Accounting touches every business, but much of the software supporting the profession was built for another era. COUNT is creating a modern platform that brings together the general ledger, practice management, payroll, automation, and AI, giving accounting firms a dramatically better way to operate and scale.

We're early, funded, and growing quickly. We're already working with global partners and ambitious accounting firms that believe the industry is ready for something different.
Joining COUNT now means helping shape the product and company while the opportunity is still wide open, with meaningful ownership, growth, and upside.

The Opportunity
COUNT is hiring a highly technical Head of Product to lead product strategy, discovery, and execution across a complex and rapidly evolving SaaS platform.
Our engineering organization includes approximately 20 developers working across numerous products, features, integrations, client requests, and technical initiatives. We have strong technical leads driving architecture and development, including leads supporting our offshore engineering teams. What we need now is a senior product leader who can create clarity across all of that work, connect Product, Engineering, QA, Support, Accounting Operations, and company leadership, and take ownership of what we build and why.
You'll work directly with accounting firms to deeply understand how they operate, where their workflows break down, and what opportunities exist to fundamentally improve the way they work. This isn't just about gathering feature requests or responding to customer feedback. We need someone who can recognize problems customers may not know how to articulate, identify opportunities they haven't thought to ask for, and help shape a product vision that moves the industry forward.

You'll take a constant stream of customer feedback, feature requests, bugs, operational needs, technical considerations, and company priorities and turn them into clearly scoped and sequenced engineering work. You'll collaborate closely with engineers to understand complexity and tradeoffs, establish priorities, drive roadmapping and planning, run standups and processes, surface blockers, and keep teams communicating and moving toward release.
Importantly, your ownership doesn't end when a feature ships. You'll be responsible for understanding whether what we built actually solved the intended problem, how customers are using it, and what we should learn or change as a result.

COUNT has strong engineering leads responsible for technical architecture and engineering leadership. This role requires enough technical depth to collaborate credibly with those engineers, understand architecture and dependencies, ask the right questions, challenge assumptions, and translate complex business needs into actionable technical requirements.
COUNT is an early-stage company transforming an industry that remains highly manual. Problems won't always arrive neatly defined. Priorities will change, information will be incomplete, and there won't always be an established process to follow. We need someone energized by that environment who can bring structure to ambiguity, think strategically about where the product should go, and take ownership without waiting for someone else to define the path.
