Overview
Microsoft’s Cloud business is expanding, and the Cloud Supply Chain (CSCP) organization is responsible for enabling the hardware infrastructure underlying this growth including AI! CSCP’s vision is to empower customers to achieve more by delivering Cloud and AI capabilities at scale. Our mission is to deliver the world's computer with an industry-leading supply chain.

The CSCP organization is responsible for traditional supply chain functions such as plan, source, make, deliver, but also manages supportability (spares), sustainability, and decommissioning of datacenter assets worldwide. We deliver the core infrastructure and foundational technologies for Microsoft's over 200 online businesses including Bing, MSN, Office 365, Xbox Live, OneDrive and the Microsoft Azure platform for external customers. Our infrastructure is supported by more than 300 datacenters around the world that enable services for more than 1 billion customers in over 90 countries.

The Supply Chain Product Management (SCPM) organization within CSCP lies at the heart of the effort to build the next-generation Cloud. We're an innovative and collaborative team of product and program managers transforming Microsoft’s Cloud and Devices Supply Chains. We design and build industry-leading systems, tools, and processes, embedding AI end-to-end for cutting-edge supply chain solutions.

Microsoft’s mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. As employees we come together with a growth mindset, innovate to empower others, and collaborate to realize our shared goals. Each day we build on our values of respect, integrity, and accountability to create a culture of inclusion where everyone can thrive at work and beyond.

Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of AI-based data platforms for high-quality, standardized, and curated data to support Microsoft’s expanding AI infrastructure.
- Drive the execution of an AI-native collaboration platform tailored for seamless interaction with external partners, suppliers, and SI’s.
- Partner with engineering and business stakeholders to deliver innovative customer-focused solutions.
- Engage with customers to understand their needs, validate scenarios, obtain feedback, and steer product decisions.
- Translate business and customer opportunities into clear and actionable product requirements and execution plans.
- Facilitate cross-functional team alignment by influencing stakeholders and achieving shared objectives.
- Prioritize investment decisions based on impact, feasibility, and alignment with strategic goals.
- Define metrics for success and track product performance, communicating key outcomes to leadership.
- Foster a culture of continual innovation, accountability, and customer-centric decision-making.
- Identify opportunities for improvement and implement strategies that drive efficiency and innovation.
