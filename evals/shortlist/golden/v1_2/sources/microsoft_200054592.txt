Overview
Microsoft AI is pushing the boundaries of technology and reimagining how people engage with information, moving beyond traditional search toward intelligent systems that help users make decisions. This is a rare opportunity to help define how Search evolves in the AI era.

In Search Places, we sit at the center of this transformation. We power how users discover, evaluate, and act on information about the physical world—from finding a restaurant to making high-confidence decisions that impact their daily lives. Our work spans classic search and emerging AI-native experiences, with a clear ambition: to build systems that don’t just retrieve information but enable decisions and complete journeys.

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to join the Search Places team to help define the future of how people discover and engage with places across traditional Search and AI experiences. Working closely with teams across MAI, you will shape how we deliver relevant, high-quality places results across these surfaces, with the opportunity to make a significant impact on how users navigate and interact with the world around them.

Responsibilities
You will define and own the roadmap for AI experiences in Search and collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to define the next generation experiences.
You will influence others to drive business impact across the organization and track success criteria for multiple feature areas across divisions. You’ll also be an expert in identifying long-term investment opportunities and engaging partners to garner support for those feature areas.
You will partner with others to collect performance metrics and form hypotheses for complex technical needs in order to improve product development in multiple areas.
You will drive cross-org alignment in a highly matrixed environment.
